## Visual Language — quick reference

**Camera grammar**

| Term | Meaning in this film |
|---|---|
| Extreme Wide | Character chhota (≈25% frame height), bahut khaali jagah |
| Medium | Full body + prop, comfortable reading distance |
| Close-up | Head + chest, face ka emotion lead karta hai |
| Top Shot | Upar se neeche — mirror ke andar ka reveal |
| Dynamic | Fast pans, impact shakes, box ke saath camera chase |
| Slow Pull Back | Dheere dheere duniya badi hoti jaati hai |

**Expression key** (eyes / mouth / brows)

| Emotion | Eyes | Mouth | Brows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relaxed | open | neutral | none |
| Curious / Excited | big | o → grin | raise |
| Strain | shut | grit | angry |
| Shock | wide | o | raise |
| Confidence | open | smile | raise |
| Defeat | dot | flat/frown | sad |
| Anger | angry | shout/grit | angry |
| Genuine joy | happy (closed arcs) | smile | none |

**Recurring visual motifs**

- White void = potential / emptiness. Sirf rain ke time grey hota hai.
- Box kabhi damage nahi hota — kyunki problem box nahi tha.
- Glow (warm yellow) = curiosity/insight. Glint sparkle = "notice this!"
- Thickness: montage ke baad stickman ke arms/legs slightly thick (≈1.38×).

## Technical notes for the animator

1. **Box kabhi damage nahi hota** — no dents, no cracks. Its indifference is the joke
   and the message. Only the *lid* ever moves (and the tiny button).
2. **The button** must be *invisible-in-plain-sight*: drawn on the right side face from
   Scene 1 onwards, but tiny (≈3% of frame at close-up). Water glint reveals it.
3. **Shakes** only on hard impacts (thuk, landings, slam, boom, crash) — 0.3–0.5 s
   exponential decay. Montage cuts use 2-frame white flashes.
4. **Rain** exists only in Scenes 6–8 (1:20–2:20) and must fully clear for the ending.
5. **Audio silence beats** are structural: 0:00–0:05, 59.75–60.15 (freeze comedy),
   114.55–117.05 (the reveal silence — *absolute zero*), fade-out at the end.
6. **Character thickness** jumps at Shot E (0:51.8) — his body literally changed
   during the montage. Keep it for all remaining scenes.
7. **Walking speeds** matter: entry is a brisk stroll (1.63 u/s), the final walk is a
   slow, contemplative one (0.30 u/s). The leg cycle must match the ground speed.
